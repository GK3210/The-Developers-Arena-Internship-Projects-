STUDENT GRADE CALCULATOR - TEST CASES
=====================================
Grading logic: A: 90-100, B: 80-89, C: 70-79, D: 60-69, F: 0-59

A. VALID INPUT (grade boundaries)
---------------------------------
No. | Marks | Expected Grade | Actual | Result
 1  | 100   | A              | A      | PASS
 2  | 95    | A              | A      | PASS
 3  | 90    | A              | A      | PASS
 4  | 89    | B              | B      | PASS
 5  | 85    | B              | B      | PASS
 6  | 80    | B              | B      | PASS
 7  | 79    | C              | C      | PASS
 8  | 75    | C              | C      | PASS
 9  | 70    | C              | C      | PASS
10  | 69    | D              | D      | PASS
11  | 65    | D              | D      | PASS
12  | 60    | D              | D      | PASS
13  | 59    | F              | F      | PASS
14  | 40    | F              | F      | PASS
15  | 0     | F              | F      | PASS

B. INVALID INPUT (handled by while loop - program re-asks)
-----------------------------------------------------------
No. | Input   | Expected Behaviour                           | Result
16  | 150     | "Marks must be between 0 and 100" -> re-ask  | PASS
17  | -5      | "Marks must be between 0 and 100" -> re-ask  | PASS
18  | abc     | "Please enter a number" -> re-ask            | PASS
19  | (blank) | "Please enter a number" -> re-ask            | PASS
20  | (blank name) | "Name cannot be empty" -> re-ask        | PASS

C. SAMPLE RUN
-------------
Enter student name: Priya
Enter marks (0-100): 85

RESULT FOR PRIYA:
Marks: 85/100
Grade: B
Message: Very Good! Keep it up!
